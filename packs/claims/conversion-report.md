### Reject-code minimum is server-side padding

§14.1's `MIN_REJECT_CODE_OPTIONS = 5` is **not** a bundle constraint. A flow
authors only the codes it expects — 1 to 3 of them, and all 23 deny flows are under 5 —
and the server pads the offered list from the pack's pool and shuffles, so a
single-option picker cannot give the answer away. Asserting the count against bundles
would have failed every deny flow in the pack.
